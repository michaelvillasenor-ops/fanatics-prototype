# Bundle — Hero moment

## What this is
Context for the few moments where the product should feel big and distinctly Fanatics Markets.
*Hero moments are earned; most surfaces aren't one.*

## Quick rules to lead with
- **Earn it.** If it's an everyday utility, use `ui-prototype`.
- **Content is the hero,** not the effects.
- **Expressive motion is allowed here;** reduced-motion parity is still required from the start.
- **No more than three flashes per second.**
- **Contrast still holds** on real, messy imagery. Use scrims or protection gradients, not blur as
  a crutch.
- **Brand color and type** follow `foundations/brand.md`.
- **Shareability:** if it's meant to be shared, design the share artifact too.
- **Anton (`marquee/large`) is the hero face** for scores and big figures; royal purple
  (`Brand/Royal` `#522cfe`) is the brand colour. FanCash purple stays reserved for FanCash.
- **Celebrate the outcome, never the risk.** A win moment shows the stake alongside the payout,
  and never nudges toward another trade. Run `responsible-gaming-check` before it ships.

## When the moment is not a hero
Settings, lists, forms, dashboards, and anything used daily.
