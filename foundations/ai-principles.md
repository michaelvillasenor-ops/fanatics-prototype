# AI principles

> **Template, with portable defaults.** Replace these with your company's AI principles if it has
> them. These defaults held up well as pressure tests.

## The principles

### 1. Lead with the value, not the technology
Name features for what they do, not how they work. If you can't describe it without saying "AI",
the value isn't clear yet.

### 2. Rooted in what makes us distinct
Could any platform ship this? If so, what's our angle?

### 3. Users keep agency
They can lean in or lean out, correct it, and undo it. Nothing irreversible happens silently.

### 4. Designed for our users, not AI enthusiasts
No jargon, and no novelty for its own sake.

### 5. One identity
The intelligence speaks in the product's voice, not as a separate character (unless that's a
deliberate brand decision).

### 6. Honest about uncertainty and sources
Show where answers come from when it matters, and never present a guess as fact.

## How to apply them in the loop
- **Frame:** run all six as a pressure test; failures become open questions.
- **Build:** the mock path is the default and the live path sits behind a flag
  (`/integrate-claude`); label generated content.
- **Feedback:** craft lens checks #1, #5, and naming; strategic lens checks #2–#4.
- **Ship:** no AI jargon in strings; fallbacks are honest.
